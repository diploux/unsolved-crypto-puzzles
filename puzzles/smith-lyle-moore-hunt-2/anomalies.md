# Anomalies: Smith, Lyle and Moore Hunt 2, Glimmer

Odd or suggestive things in this puzzle that are not yet resolved into an answer.
Status values: `unexplained`, `promoted`, `explained`, `dismissed`. Nothing is
deleted.

Unlike the cipher puzzles in this catalogue, the unsolved part here is not a
transform but three riddle answers. So an anomaly here is usually a clue whose
target is not yet pinned, rather than a measurement that does not fit.

---

## A1. The band said their next hunt would reuse an unsolved encoding from the first

**Status**: unexplained

On their own forum the band said that an encoding in Hunt 1 was never solved,
that it was "referenced on the treasure hunt page with the rainbow road stuff",
and that they would "probably use a similar method to encode something into our
next release". The next release is this hunt.

Glimmer's video is a literal rainbow kaleidoscope, and the night page carries a
rainbow comb jelly. If the same colour-to-symbol scheme is in use, the visuals
may carry data rather than just theme.

This is the single most promising avenue that is not a password guess, because
it is a mechanism the authors say they used, not one inferred. The blocker is
that the Hunt 1 "rainbow road" scheme was itself never published, so the mapping
has to be recovered first.

**Would explain it**: recovering the Hunt 1 rainbow-road encoding from its old
page or forum history, then applying it to Glimmer's rainbow visuals.

## A2. The West riddle misspells one word deliberately

**Status**: unexplained

The West page reads "unbrida​led rage". The intended word is "unbridled". The
inserted letters make "bridal", which points at The Princess Bride, whose Dread
Pirate Roberts is the alias Ross Ulbricht used, and Ulbricht is one of the six
pirates the riddle names. It is not an anagram of any word.

So the misspelling is a pointer, and it connects two of the six names to each
other. The word that unites all six is still not pinned. The six are Blackbeard,
Shawn Fanning, Lars Ulrich, Ross Ulbricht, Gottfrid Svartholm and Fredrik Neij:
a historical pirate, the founder of Napster, the drummer who sued Napster, the
Silk Road operator, and two Pirate Bay founders.

**Would explain it**: the one word or name they share, in lowercase, opening the
West gate.

## A3. The West background is a specific film ship, not generic pirate art

**Status**: unexplained

The image behind the West riddle is the Inferno, One-Eyed Willy's galleon from
The Goonies, identified rather than assumed. Meaningful images in this hunt are
the answer, by the band's own stated design, so this choice is likely load
bearing.

Every direct Goonies term has been tried and rejected, so the ship is either
pointing past itself, at the theme of buried treasure claimed by outsiders, or
it is one input among several the six names also share.

**Would explain it**: a reading that connects the Goonies "never say die"
outsiders-claim-the-treasure theme to the six named pirates.

## A4. The East lock sits behind a near-death, life-review scene

**Status**: unexplained

The locked East page describes hydrogen narcosis and images flashing before the
eyes, ending "Is this the end?". The lead-up used a Gandalf reference, and one
earlier page's password was `youshallpass47`, so the "is this the end" question
reads as a callback to the Gandalf line about the road going ever on,
from the same source.

Every exact phrasing of that line has been tried and rejected, along with the
whole near-death and cosmic-end field.

**Would explain it**: the single named reference the band intends for "the end",
in lowercase. Their design uses one exact reference per gate, so the answer is a
specific title or line, not a concept.

## A5. The South master gate opens an entire further sequence

**Status**: unexplained

The South lock is not just the end of the castaway chain. Its password is a
master key that also opens the whole downstream South sequence in one step,
which makes South the highest-value of the three locks: solving it may yield more
than one word.

The gate's own text points at the Professor, the sixth castaway, whose real name
is Roy Hinkley. Every form of that has been rejected, and a community hint says
to "use a different title".

**Would explain it**: the "different title" for the Professor, in Title Case.
Given the band's habit of using the obscure real name, the answer is likely a
specific alternative name rather than a role.

---

## A6. The unattributed video in the East branch

**Status**: unexplained

The life-review video shows the band's own travel memories, Amalfi, Saint
Petersburg, Greece, Malibu wildflowers, and an Apollo Saturn V launch. It was
checked for a hidden text frame, for Morse in the audio, and for steganography,
and none was found. The kaleidoscope transition colours are noisy with no clean
code.

It is recorded here because in Hunt 1 the band did hide seed words in single
video frames and Morse in an inverted audio mix, so a video that appears clean
is not automatically empty. It may also connect to the rainbow-road lead in A1.

**Would explain it**: either a frame or audio channel found on a better capture,
or confirmation that this video is theme only.

## Dismissed

### D1. Passwords are always lowercase

An early working assumption, on which roughly 1,560 West guesses were submitted
in lowercase. Refuted directly: on two already-open gates the correct word
succeeds only in its exact casing and fails in lowercase and uppercase. This
invalidated a large block of West negatives and is why the Title Case retry is a
live lead. Dismissed 2026-08-17.

### D2. A second cover image was a Hunt 2 clue

A collage that looked like a Glimmer cover turned out to be a Hunt 1 teaser
predating this hunt. Dismissed 2026-08-17.

### D3. An alternate audio mix carries Morse, as in Hunt 1

No alternate mix is distributed anywhere. The only public high-resolution
Glimmer audio matches its own official master. Dismissed 2026-08-17.

### D4. The site's own hint mechanism helps here

The band gives hints by email, but the mechanism covers only the first three
introductory steps, which are already solved. It offers no lever on the three
locks. Dismissed 2026-08-17.
